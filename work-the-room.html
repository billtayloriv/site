Questions about the event

Who should come?

Anyone who wants to build their professional network: founders, sales and customer success leaders, consultants, service providers, and people looking for their next opportunity.

I hate networking events. Is this different?

That is exactly who we built it for. The focus is on conversation and genuine connection, not on pitching everyone in sight.

What should I bring?

Business cards, anything that helps promote yourself or your business, and a resume if you're job hunting. Most importantly, bring yourself. If you're having trouble warming up, our connectors are happy to introduce you to a few people to get you started.

Can I sponsor or partner with the event?

Yes, we would like to hear from you. Send us a note and we will talk.
